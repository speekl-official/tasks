The human output drops what a reader never needs: `resume` prints the last log entry as `mine` does (its text, then the author and the age), `log --since` and `tasks log <task>` head each entry with its time and author without the session token, `tasks log <task> "<text>"` confirms with the text it wrote, and a takeover names the superseded claimant with the age of its last heartbeat instead of its token and timestamp. The JSON and `log.md` carry every token and timestamp as before.
